Paint Hide Run! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Paint Hide Run! is operated by Polyque Games. This policy explains what data the Game handles.

1. Data We Process

  • Sign in with Apple: a stable anonymous user identifier (and, if you share it, your name) used to keep your profile, coins and purchases.
  • Gameplay data: nickname, scores, coins and cosmetic ownership, stored on the device and linked to your Apple identifier.
  • Multiplayer networking: real-time position/game actions are relayed through Photon (Exit Games GmbH) servers during matches; they are not stored after the session.
  • Advertising: optional rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers per its own policy.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple; we never see payment details.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your email, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not sell personal data.

3. Account Deletion

Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your account identity and associated data from the device. You can also revoke Sign in with Apple access in your Apple ID settings.

4. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is technically required to operate the Game.

5. Third Parties

Photon (multiplayer relay), Google AdMob (ads), Apple (sign-in and purchases). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.
